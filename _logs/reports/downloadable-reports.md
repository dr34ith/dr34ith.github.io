## A Quick Guide to My Burp Suite Workflow

I use the FoxyProxy extension in Chrome to make BurpSuite easier to work with. It lets me quickly turn interception on or off and focus only on the websites I want to test. When I find something interesting, I dig deeper using Browser Developer Tools and manual request/response analysis to fully understand what's happening.
